# KDA Garage artwork

The garage mesh, shutter, roof extractor, sign and six textures are original procedural KDA artwork by Roderic Systems. Source: `source/assets/kda-garage/build_garage.py`. No external building model or texture inputs are used.

The catalog identifier is `KDAGarage`; assets are under `Garage/`. The closed exterior uses solid shell colliders. The separate textured workshop interior is also KDA artwork.
